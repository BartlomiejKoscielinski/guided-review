# Autopilot decisions (log)

Decisions the autopilot took on its own where the spec and mocks were silent (docs/process/AUTOPILOT.md §3.2). They are final (`decided`) and nothing waits for the owner (owner, 2026-10-14): the autopilot picks the best option for the user (spec and mocks first, then consistency with the existing screens and the design system, then established UI/UX principles) and gives its reason after "—" in the Chosen cell. One row per criterion: its questions are the points (1), (2), … of the cells. The owner may still veto at any time: status `vetoed` (the whole row) or `vetoed (2)` (only the points named); the next `backlog.mjs sync` adds a rework item. Decisions move into the docs (01–08) with the next item that touches them.
